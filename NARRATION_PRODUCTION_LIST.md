# Werewolf Ultimate — Narration Production List

Record each item as a separate MP3 file. Where a **MODERATOR PAUSE** is shown, the app should stop and wait for the moderator to confirm that the action is complete. Do not add a long silence to the recording.

## General narration

- [ ] `01-welcome.mp3`
  - “Welcome to the village. By day, the village debates and votes. By night, hidden roles wake and act. Keep your card secret, listen carefully, and decide whom to trust before the wolves take control.”

- [ ] `02-how-to-play.mp3`
  - “First, deal the physical role cards. Each player secretly checks their card. When night falls, everyone closes their eyes and the moderator follows the prompts while active roles silently make their choices. At dawn, reveal the victims and discuss what happened. The village may eliminate one suspect each day. Continue through night and day until a faction completes its objective.”

- [ ] `03-game-begins.mp3`

- [x] `04-new-player-strategy.mp3`

## Recording notes

- Export as MP3.
- Keep volume, voice, speed, and microphone distance consistent across every file.
- Leave a short natural gap at the beginning and end, but do not add long action pauses.
- Use the exact filenames above so the files can be connected to the app automatically.
- Record optional roles even if they are not used in every game; the app will play only the clips required by the selected physical cards.
