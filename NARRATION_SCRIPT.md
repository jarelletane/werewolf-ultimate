# Narration recording list

Generate each line as a separate MP3. Use a consistent older English male voice: low, warm, grave, measured, and theatrical without becoming difficult to understand. Leave roughly 250 ms of silence at the beginning and 500 ms at the end.

Suggested delivery: 0.85–0.9× conversational speed, restrained emotion, clean studio sound, no baked-in music or reverb. Export mono MP3 at 128 kbps or better. The app can mix ambience underneath the clean voice track.

## Dynamic names

Player names cannot be pre-generated reliably because each group enters its own names. Keep the tragedy announcement as separate intro and outro clips, then either use device speech for the names between them or display the names without speaking them. The four fixed winner clips above avoid the same problem for victory announcements.
